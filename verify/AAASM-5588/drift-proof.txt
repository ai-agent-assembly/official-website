4. THE STDLIB MANIFEST SCANNER AGREES WITH PyYAML
================================================================
The scanner in scripts/trust-evidence.py reads YAML with the standard
library, because the CI job running the claim gates has no PyYAML. It was
cross-checked against yaml.safe_load of the same bytes over the WHOLE
projection — counts, per-area distributions and the four platform rows,
not only id/domain/coverage. Result: FULL PROJECTION AGREES.
The cross-check also caught a real defect before it shipped: the first
version attributed policy_context items to platform, giving P1 three
platforms, two of which were policy names. A closed-enum guard on
platform values now makes that failure loud instead of published.
